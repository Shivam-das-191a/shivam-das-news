## Goal
The build stops when a `rel` word on a link, an area or a `<link>` tag is a typo of a real word (`noopner`, `nofolow`), when a `<link>` that needs a file has no `href`, and when a `preload` has no `as` or an `as` that is not real; it warns on a font preload with no `crossorigin` and on `rel="opener"`.

## Why it matters
A browser never reports a typo in `rel`: it silently ignores the unknown word. So `rel="noopner"` loses the safety of `noopener`, `rel="nofolow"` loses the SEO hint, and `rel="stylesheeet"` loads no style. A `preload` with no `as` is thrown away, and a font preload with no `crossorigin` makes the browser fetch the font twice. Links and hints written by hand in a post or a custom block (PLAN 37) can carry these words.
