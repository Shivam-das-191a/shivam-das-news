## Goal
The build stops when the top picture of an article is not high priority or is lazy-loaded, and warns about article pictures that are not lazy.

## Why it matters
POLISH lists "Hero image preloaded and `fetchpriority="high"`" and "All other images lazy-loaded". The page template does it today, but nothing kept it that way when someone changes a template or writes a picture by hand.

## Steps
1. New `img-priority-lib.mjs` with `checkImgLoading`.
2. `validate-html.mjs`: call it for every built page with the page type.
3. New test `img-priority.test.ts`.
4. Deliberate mistakes, one at a time.
5. Version 0.101.102, changelog, `CODING_PLAN.md`, `INCOMPLETE.md`, `UPDATE_NOTE.md`.

## Done when
- `npm test` passes and `npm run build` ends with `Validation passed.`
- An article whose top picture has no `fetchpriority="high"`, or is lazy, fails the gate; an article picture that is not lazy only prints a warning; a good article passes.
