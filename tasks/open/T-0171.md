## Goal
The build warns when the text of an English built page has a space just inside a curly double quote, no space before an opening curly double quote that follows a word, or no space after a closing curly double quote that is followed by a word, and never stops the build for them.

## Why it matters
Text typed fast, pasted from Word or imported from Blogger often has a quote with a space inside it, a quote glued to the word before it (`said` then the opening mark) or glued to the word after it (the closing mark then `then`). A reader sees each one at once and trusts the site a little less. Nothing in the build read the gaps around quotes.

## Steps
1. New `site/scripts/quote-space-check-lib.mjs` with `checkQuoteSpacing(html)` (reads the title and the body text of a page whose `lang` is English or not given; any other language writes its quote marks another way, so that page is not read; an inline tag leaves no gap, a block tag ends the run, a `br` does not; comments and script, style, template, textarea, pre, code, kbd, samp, svg, noscript and select blocks skipped; attributes and the meta description not read; one message per kind with a count; no errors).
2. Kinds: a space just inside a curly double quote (after U+201C before a letter or digit, or before U+201D after a letter, digit or mark); no space before an opening curly double quote that follows a letter; no space after a closing curly double quote that is followed by a word of 2 or more letters (A to Z).
3. Left out: the straight quote, the single quote and the apostrophe, a German style quote (low opening mark, curly closing mark), a gap written as `&nbsp;`, a quote at the start or end of a paragraph, a quote next to a bracket, a dash or a mark, code, a page in another language.
4. `site/scripts/validate-html.mjs`: import it, run it on every built page, name it in the first comment line.
5. New test `quote-space-check.test.ts` (57 cases, with a fake dist folder run of the real gate).
6. Run the whole test suite and the real build, then the gate on a throw away copy of the real build with the three slips added.
7. Version 0.101.173, changelog, `CODING_PLAN.md`, `INCOMPLETE.md`, `UPDATE_NOTE.md`.

## Done when
- `npm test` passes and `npm run build` ends with `Validation passed.` and no new message.
- A page with a quote with spaces inside, a quote glued to the word before and a quote glued to the word after gives three warnings that name the page; the build still passes.
- Clean quotes, a quote at the start of a paragraph, `&nbsp;` gaps, a German style quote, straight quotes, a page with `lang="sv"` and text in `pre` or `code` pass with no message.
