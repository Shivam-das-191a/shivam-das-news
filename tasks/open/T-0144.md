## Goal
The build stops when a clickable picture is wired wrongly: a `usemap` that names no map, a map with no name or a repeated name, an `<area>` with an unknown shape or bad `coords`, or a linked `<area>` with no `alt`; it warns on a map nobody uses, an `<area>` outside a `<map>` and an old shape name.

## Why it matters
A picture with `usemap="#shop"` and no `<map name="shop">` has no clickable parts, an `<area>` with coords of the wrong length is ignored, and an `<area>` with a link and no `alt` is a link with no name. The dashboard preview shows nothing wrong. Old Blogger posts (XML import) and a snippet pasted by hand (PLAN 37) can carry them.

1. New `site/scripts/map-check-lib.mjs` with `checkMaps(html)` (one pass over the img, map and area tags; comments, script, style and template skipped).
