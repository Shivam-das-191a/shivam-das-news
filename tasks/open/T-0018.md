## Goal
A post can carry its own share card (`ogTitle`, `ogDescription`, `ogImage`) and the page head writes it; the rules for the share card, the canonical address and the schema type exist in `listing.js` and are tested. No screen yet (Part 5b).

## Why it matters
The Advanced fold of PLAN 33.2 needs data, rules and a page head that obey them before a screen can show them. Rules first, tested alone, so the screen only shows them and cannot get them wrong (same order as Parts 1 and 2).

## Steps
1. Read `NEWS_WEBSITE_PLAN.md` 33.2 and `LOGICS.md` Section 21.
2. Add the three fields to `src/content.config.ts` and to the front matter order.
3. Add `shareValues` to `src/lib/seo.ts`; make `Base.astro` and the article page use it.
4. Add `shareCard`, `checkCanonical`, `checkSchemaType` and `advancedErrors` to `listing.js`; add them to `hardErrors`.
5. Add the action `post:canonical` (admin, editor) and the content check rules.
6. Write the tests, make deliberate mistakes to prove they fail, build a real page and read its head.
7. Bump the version, write the changelog, update `CODING_PLAN.md` and `INCOMPLETE.md` (`VERSIONING.md`).

## Done when
- `npm test`, `npm run build` and `npm run preflight` pass; older tests pass unchanged.
- A published post with `ogTitle`, `ogDescription` and `ogImage` shows them in `og:` and `twitter:` tags; a post without them shows exactly what it showed before.
- `check:content` refuses a bad canonical, a share title over 70, a share description over 320 and a missing share picture.
