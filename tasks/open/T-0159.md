## Goal
The build stops when a page has a second `<html>`, `<head>` or `<body>`, a `<head>` after the `<body>` starts, a tag or text in the head that a head may not hold, a canonical link in the body, or a `<title>` only in the body; it warns on a second `<title>` in the body, a `<meta>` with `name`, `property`, `http-equiv` or `charset` in the body, content after `</body>` or `</html>`, and a page with no `<head>` or no `<body>` tag.

## Why it matters
A `<div>`, a picture or text in the head ends the head early, so the canonical link, the share tags and the structured data after it land in the body and may not be read. A whole HTML page pasted into a post (an old Blogger export, a copied snippet) gives a second `<html>`, `<head>` and `<body>` that the browser ignores. No message shows either way. The noscript check (T-0158) covers one cause only; nothing read the skeleton of the page as a whole.

## Steps
1. New `site/scripts/structure-check-lib.mjs` with `checkStructure(html)` (comments and script, style, template, noscript and textarea blocks skipped; everything inside an svg or math skipped).
2. `site/scripts/validate-html.mjs`: import it, run it on every built page, name it in the first comment line.
3. New test `structure-check.test.ts` (50 cases, with a fake dist folder run of the real gate).
4. Run the whole test suite and the real build, then the gate on a throw away copy of the real build with bad markup added.
