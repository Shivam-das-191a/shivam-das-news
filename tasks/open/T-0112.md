## Goal
The build stops when the page landmarks are in the wrong place: the h1 outside `main`, a `main` inside a header, footer, nav, aside or article, and a story page that has no `article`, has two, has its `article` outside `main`, or has its h1 outside the `article`.

## Why it matters
POLISH (Accessibility and Mobile) and BUGS group U ask for an article landmark on a post page. Until now the build checked only that there is one `main`, a header and a footer (v0.101.101, `page-basics-lib.mjs`). A story page could lose its `article` and a screen reader user would not find the story. The skip link goes to `#main`; an h1 outside `main` is skipped by it.

## Steps
1. New `site/scripts/landmarks-lib.mjs` with `checkLandmarks(html, type)`.
2. `site/scripts/validate-html.mjs`: run it on every built page, with the value of `data-page-type`.
3. The fake story page of `img-priority.test.ts` gets an `article` around its h1 and body (one inserted tag pair), because a story page with no article is now an error.
4. New test `landmarks.test.ts` (32 cases, with a fake dist folder run of the real gate and a read of the two real templates).
5. Run the gate on today's real build, then on throw away copies with the real build broken.
6. Version 0.101.115, changelog, `CODING_PLAN.md`, `INCOMPLETE.md`, `UPDATE_NOTE.md`.

## Done when
- `npm test` passes and `npm run build` ends with `Validation passed.` and no new message.
- An h1 outside `main`, a `main` inside another landmark, and a story page with no article, two articles, an article outside `main` or an h1 outside its article fail the gate.
- An `aside` with no label only warns.
