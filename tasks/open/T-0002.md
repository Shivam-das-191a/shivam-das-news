## Goal
Five real, original, accurate articles are published, so the home page, the category pages and the author page have content.

## Why it matters
The site shows "No stories yet". No search engine or ad network can approve or index an empty site, and the author page cannot exist until there is a post.

## Steps
1. Owner: choose five topics and write or approve the facts and sources for each. An AI may help with structure, headlines, grammar and the front matter; it may not invent facts, quotes or sources.
2. Create each post as in `AI_OPERATIONS.md` Section 3 (image, `imageAlt`, description 50 to 160 characters, category, author).
3. Publish one at a time (Section 4). Check each live page.
4. Check the author page and `sitemap-authors.xml`.

## Done when
- Five posts have `status: published`, each with image, alt text, description, a real source line where needed, and no emoji.
- The home page lists them; `/author/shivam-das/` exists; `sitemap-authors.xml` lists the author.
- The build is green.

## Evidence
(Five live addresses with the status seen, the author page address, the sitemap line.)

## If it goes wrong
Set the post back to `draft`, or `git revert` the commit.

## Stop and ask the owner if
- A fact, a quote or a photo's source is unclear.
- The topic could harm a real person (law, health, accusations).
