## Goal
The build stops when an `<input>` has a `type` that is not a real input type, when a `<button>` has a `type` that is not submit, reset or button, or when a `maxlength` or `minlength` is not a whole number; it warns on a `<button>` inside a `<form>` that has no type.

## Why it matters
A browser never reports a typo such as `type="emial"` or `type="sumbit"`. The e-mail box silently becomes a plain text box (no e-mail keyboard on a phone, no e-mail check), and a mistyped button silently submits the form. A length such as `maxlength="abc"` is ignored without a word. The contact form, the notifications form and any form the owner writes by hand in a post (PLAN 37) must do what they say.
