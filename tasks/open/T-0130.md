## Goal
The build stops when the `srcset` of an `<img>` or `<source>` has an empty value, a descriptor that is not real, a mix of `w` and `x`, the same descriptor twice or an address on `http://`, and when a `sizes` is empty or does not end with a real length; it warns on a `w` srcset with no `sizes` and on a `sizes` that does nothing.

## Why it matters
A browser never reports a broken `srcset`: it drops the bad candidate or the whole list. A phone then downloads the big picture, or the page falls back to the plain `src`. The usual slip is `sizes="100%"` (a percent is not a length), and a `w` srcset with no `sizes` makes the browser assume the picture fills the whole screen. The hosted pictures get their `srcset` and `sizes` from `picture-set.ts` (PLAN 34.5), and a picture written by hand in a post (PLAN 37) can carry its own.
