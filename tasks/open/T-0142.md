## Goal
The build stops when an attribute that must hold a whole number holds something else (`width="auto"` on a picture, `colspan="abc"`, `start="x"`); it warns on a size written as `640px` or `100%`, on a `colspan` or `span` out of range and on a `rows`, `cols` or `size` of 0.

## Why it matters
The browser quietly repairs these typos, so the page looks different from what the writer meant. A width and height that is not a whole number of pixels stops the browser from reserving the space before a picture loads. Old Blogger posts (XML import) and a snippet pasted by hand (PLAN 37) carry them, and the dashboard preview shows nothing wrong.

1. New `site/scripts/number-check-lib.mjs` with `checkNumbers(html)` (one pass over the img, video, canvas, td, th, col, colgroup, ol, li, textarea, input and select tags; comments, script, style and template skipped; an iframe is left out).
