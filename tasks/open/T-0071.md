## Goal
A small pure function `imageSize(bytes)` that gives the pixel width and height of a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP from its first bytes, and `null` for anything else.

## Why it matters
The width limit (`maxWidth` of PLAN 34.10) cannot be built until the project can read a picture size. This is the first small step. Nothing calls it yet, so nothing changes today.

## Steps
1. `site/scripts/image-size-lib.mjs`: `imageSize` (PNG header, JPEG start-of-frame segment, GIF header, WebP lossy, lossless and extended).
2. `site/tests/unit/image-size.test.ts`: real pictures made by `sharp`, a JPEG with extra segments, the `null` cases, then five deliberate mistakes.
3. Bump the version, write the changelog, update `CODING_PLAN.md`, `INCOMPLETE.md`, `UPDATE_NOTE.md`.
