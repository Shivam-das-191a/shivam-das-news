## Goal
The build stops when a `<noscript>` in the head holds anything but `link`, `style` and `meta` (or text), or when a `<noscript>` is never closed; it warns on an empty `<noscript>`, a `<script>` inside a `<noscript>` and a `</noscript>` with no opening one.

## Why it matters
A picture or a paragraph inside a head `<noscript>` ends the head early for a reader whose JavaScript is off, so the canonical link, the share tags and the structured data land in the body and are lost. A `<noscript>` never closed turns the rest of the page into text for a reader whose JavaScript is on. No message shows either way. A pixel snippet pasted by hand (PLAN 37) or an old Blogger page can carry these. Nothing read `<noscript>` before.

## Steps
1. New `site/scripts/noscript-check-lib.mjs` with `checkNoscript(html)` (comments and script, style and template blocks outside a noscript skipped).
