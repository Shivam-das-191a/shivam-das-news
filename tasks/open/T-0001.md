## Goal
`config/publisher.json` has a true `address`, `foundedYear` and `fundingSummary`, and the required pages (About, Privacy, Terms, Contact, Editorial policy, Corrections and the rest) exist and are linked in the footer.

## Why it matters
Today the footer has no About, Privacy or Contact link and the home page is empty. Reviewers of AdSense and Google News look for these pages first.

## Steps
1. Owner: write the three facts, true and short. `address` is the real publisher address (or the contact address you are willing to show publicly). `foundedYear` is the year the site or company really started. `fundingSummary` is one plain sentence on how the site is paid for.
2. Owner: put them in `site/config/publisher.json` (dashboard Settings, or GitHub), or send them in the chat for the AI to enter.
3. Run `npm run pages` in `site/` (or ask the AI) so the pages are generated.
4. Open the live site and check the footer links.

## Done when
- `publisher.json` has no empty required field.
- The footer of the live site links to About, Privacy, Terms, Contact and Editorial policy, and each page opens.
- `npm test` and `npm run build` pass.

## Evidence
(Commit link, the list of pages generated, the live footer seen.)

## If it goes wrong
Revert the commit; the old footer returns.

## Stop and ask the owner if
- Any of the three facts is unknown. Never invent an address, a year or a funding statement.
- The legal text needs a lawyer's view.
