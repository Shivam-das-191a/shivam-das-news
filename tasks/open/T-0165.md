## Goal
The build warns about a title or heading in all capital letters and about `!!` or `??` marks in a title or heading, and never stops the build for them.

## Why it matters
A news site is judged by its headlines. A title written in capitals or ending in `!!!` reads as clickbait to readers and to Google News and Discover, and Google often rewrites such a title. A post imported from Blogger or pasted by hand often has one. Nothing in the build read the style of a headline.

## Steps
1. New `site/scripts/shout-check-lib.mjs` with `checkShouting(html)` (reads `title`, `h1`, `h2`, `h3`; comments and script, style, template, textarea, pre, svg and noscript blocks skipped; one message per kind with a count; no errors).
2. All capitals means 12 or more letters (A to Z) and no small letter; marks mean `!!`, `??` or three or more `!` and `?` in a row.
3. `site/scripts/validate-html.mjs`: import it, run it on every built page, name it in the first comment line.
4. New test `shout-check.test.ts` (39 cases, with a fake dist folder run of the real gate).
5. Run the whole test suite and the real build, then the gate on a throw away copy of the real build with a loud heading added.
6. Version 0.101.167, changelog, `CODING_PLAN.md`, `INCOMPLETE.md`, `UPDATE_NOTE.md`.

## Done when
- `npm test` passes and `npm run build` ends with `Validation passed.` and no new message.
- A page with `<h2>SHOCKING TRUTH ABOUT THE BUDGET</h2>` gives a warning that names the page; the build still passes.
- `FAQ`, `FBI RAID`, one `!`, a `?!` pair, a heading with one small letter and an svg `<title>` pass with no message.
